Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy explains what data Kanal Upload ("the app") uses, why, and where it is stored. The app runs locally on your own computer. It has no server of its own and does not send your data to the operator or to any third party other than TikTok.

1. Who is responsible

Kaan Aydemir, email: lincerof32@gmail.com ("we").

2. Data the app uses

When you connect a TikTok account and grant permission, the app receives from TikTok:

  • Basic account information (user.info.basic): your TikTok user ID for this app (open_id), display name and avatar.
  • Account statistics (user.info.stats): follower count, following count, total likes and number of videos.
  • Your public videos (video.list): video ID, title or description, cover image, link, publish date, and view, like, comment and share counts.
  • Login tokens: an access token and a refresh token issued by TikTok, so you do not have to log in again every day.

When you choose to send a video as a draft (video.upload), the app uploads that video file to TikTok. Only videos you select are uploaded.

3. Why the data is used

  • To show which TikTok account is connected to which of your channels.
  • To show your channel and video statistics on your dashboard.
  • To upload videos you select to your TikTok inbox as drafts.

The data is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose, and it is never sold.

4. Where the data is stored

All data listed above is stored only on the computer where you run the app. We have no access to it. Data is exchanged only between your computer and TikTok's servers (open.tiktokapis.com) over an encrypted connection. TikTok's own processing is governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy.

5. How long the data is kept

Data is kept until you disconnect the TikTok account in the app or delete the app's data folder. Login tokens expire automatically if they are not renewed (access tokens after 24 hours, refresh tokens after 365 days).

6. Revoking access

You can disconnect a TikTok account in the app at any time. You can also revoke access in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Manage app permissions.

7. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses when the site is visited; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. Because all app data stays on your own computer, you can view and delete it yourself at any time. For questions, contact us at the email above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

9. Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
