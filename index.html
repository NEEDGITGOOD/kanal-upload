Kanal Upload

A desktop tool for creators who run several short-video channels. Send finished videos to TikTok as drafts and keep followers and views for every channel in one place.

What it does

  • Connect TikTok: log in with your TikTok account (TikTok Login Kit). Each channel is connected once.
  • Send as draft: pick a finished video and send it to your TikTok inbox. You review, edit and post it yourself in the TikTok app. Kanal Upload never publishes anything on its own.
  • Channel stats: see followers, likes and your videos' views, likes, comments and shares on one dashboard.

How it works

Kanal Upload runs on your own computer as a local app with a browser interface. Your TikTok login tokens, your videos and your statistics stay on that computer. There is no Kanal Upload server and no account to create.

You can disconnect a TikTok account in the app at any time, or remove access in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Manage app permissions.
